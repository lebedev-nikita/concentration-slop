# Interface

A native macOS utility with a borderless, shadowless video window with continuous 14-point rounded corners and a transparent background. Actual video fills the window with its own aspect ratio; system typography, SF Symbols and adaptive colors are reserved for menus and setup states. Preserve uncropped gameplay.

Hover reveals a compact control strip inside the video. All four corners are always-available, invisible resize areas with matching diagonal cursors. The strip offers pause, link, app binding and hide. Controls disappear on pointer exit without changing the video layout. Drag uncovered video to move, double-click to pause, or right-click for controls; the menu bar exposes the same actions. Size changes happen directly at any corner, without size buttons or presets. Continuous corner resizing preserves the video ratio and opposite-corner anchor, within the screen and minimum size. Binding attaches to one work window. Fixed insets from the nearest edges follow its geometry and keeps video within its bounds; shrinking the host may temporarily shrink video without losing the preferred size. Drag and resize use the same placement controller. Link entry uses a separate focused native window. Loading is cancellable; errors offer retry and another link.

The panel does not activate the app or become main; it becomes key only when needed. An optional persisted app binding selects its current visible work window. Window identity remains fixed while that window exists, including when minimized or off-Space; rebinding selects another window. Presentation requires both the app to be active and the attached window to be visible on the current Space, so a stale activation cannot flash video onto another desktop. Bound panels belong to a single Space and participate in native desktop transitions; they must not remain above the transition while waiting for app activation notifications. Unbound video stays available across Spaces. Actual Space membership and window occlusion also govern playback; hiding stops it without changing the user's pause choice, including video resolution completing while hidden. Manual hiding lasts until explicitly shown again. WindowServer supplies identity and Space visibility; Accessibility supplies logical window geometry so native desktop animation is applied exactly once. Binding requires the macOS Accessibility permission. Read geometry only, without titles or pixels. Geometry IPC runs on a bounded serial background queue during native drag/resize and stops while the app is inactive.
